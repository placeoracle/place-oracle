Star positions and magnitudes: Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Edition (Hoffleit & Warren 1991), distributed by the Astronomical Data Center; public domain. Selected entries (magnitude <= 5.0) adapted from https://github.com/isene/starmap/blob/master/data/stars.csv .
Star names: IAU Catalog of Star Names, via the same data compilation.
Observation-point coordinates: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/ (site IDs 1195, 934, 662, 1037, 551, 98, 304, 181, 1152). They are reference points for broad properties, not exact viewing spots.

Constellation line data: d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn, https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial , adapted via https://github.com/isene/starmap/blob/master/data/constellations.csv .
